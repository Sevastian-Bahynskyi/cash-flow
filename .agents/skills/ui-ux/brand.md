# Aura

Aura is Cashflow's native design direction. Money summaries use a living field with Barlow Condensed figures. Lists sit on a quiet black ground with readable amounts and subdued secondary text.

- Cycle opens on the current salary cycle. Earlier cycles sit to its left. One Budget status page sits to its right. Keep history ordered oldest to newest and show the next older dot as the user goes back.
- Put the budget amount and state on the parent. Indent child spending and let people open the charges behind it.
- A budget's colour identifies its parent category. Its children, budget meter, spending charts and transaction icons share that colour. Keep amounts neutral and use text to identify ahead, over, and used-up states. Amber for shared identity and coral for over-budget status retain their separate meanings.
- Use Lucide outlines across the dock, budget rows and Activity. Use the category icon with a label and the parent budget accent. Keep navigation glyphs neutral and use a stronger stroke and the dock highlight for the selected tab.
- Motion follows a swipe or an action and settles promptly. Use a selection tick on a discrete page or option change. Test the touch response on an iPhone.
