# Engraved Note

**Idea:** the month is a banknote you hold: the money left until payday is engraved on a sepia note with a guilloche rosette holding the days to payday, the shared account is a second, slate note, and everything else is set like a well-typeset statement around them.

## References and what each gives

- **Apple Card and Wallet (applecard-02, -06):** the account as a tactile object you hold, and a single paid-against-remaining bar. Here: the sepia note on Home and the one budget meter on a category.
- **Revolut (revolut-02, -04):** a huge balance on a rich surface, and a joint account that feels like two people. Here: the hero figure on the note, the shared note with two monograms and the 60/40 top-up bar.
- **Monarch (monarch-01, -03):** an editorial serif voice for headlines and figures; budgets as bars with spent and left in words. Here: Source Serif for titles and amounts, the all-budgets table.
- **YNAB (ynab-03, -05):** the amount left in a coloured pill on each row; overspending as a red block with the next step. Here: green and red pills in All budgets; the red Over budget block on Home that opens the category.
- **Copilot (copilot-03):** a budget table in aligned columns. Here: category, spent, left, right-aligned, with the accountant's double rule under a difference.

## Colour rule and type

Banknote inks. Intaglio inks, the two classic one-colour banknote inks: sepia is personal money, slate is the shared account with Anna, and both colours mean exactly that everywhere (cards, row tags, chart segments, dots). Two status inks only: green for money left or money in, vermilion for over, always with the word "over" or a sign. Everything else is ink on a cool grey paper. Dark theme keeps the same inks on a night ground, each lifted for contrast, not inverted.

Type: Source Serif 4 for titles and all figures (tabular in columns, proportional in the hero), Hanken Grotesk for labels and UI text. The engraving on the notes is drawn in SVG (wave lines and a guilloche rosette), no images.

## Why it is unmistakably money and my month

The first thing on screen is a note that says how much is left until payday, with the 31 days of the November cycle printed along its edge, today marked, and the daily amount until then. Shared and personal are two different notes in two different inks; money moved to the shared account is shown as moved, not spent, with a dashed mark.

## Least sure of

Whether the serif plus engraving reads as premium print or tips toward the editorial look the owner warned about; the notes carry a lot of the identity, and on desktop the shared note sits below the fold on Home.
