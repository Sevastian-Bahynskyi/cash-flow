# Signal Board

**Idea:** the cycle is a departure board: a full-width band in the colour of the cycle's state holds the money left and the days to payday, and everything under it is a strict timetable of ruled rows and condensed figures.

## References and what each gives

- **Monarch (monarch-03, -02):** the coloured "left to budget" band at the top, and the Sankey that shows where every krone went. Here: the green band (red once the budget is spent) and the Compare screen, where personal and shared money flow into categories.
- **Linear (linear-01, -02):** calm, dense lists with one column of status marks. Here: shared rows carry an amber dot and moved money a dashed square; personal is the default and unmarked.
- **Copilot (copilot-03, -05):** aligned budget columns, and bars where only the bad one is red. Here: the all-budgets table, and the category chart where only the part above the budget turns red.
- **YNAB (ynab-05, -09):** overspending as a red block, and plain bars with a verdict sentence above. Here: the red Over budget block on a category, and the one-sentence answer above the Sankey.
- **Revolut (revolut-04):** the joint account as two people. Here: the shared section split into You and Anna, each with what they put in and their share.

## Colour rule and type

Signal colours, as on a station board. The band shows the state of the cycle: green while the budget holds, red once it is spent. Amber is the shared account with Anna and nothing else. Red is reserved for over. Money in carries a plus and green. Ink and paper carry everything else, with 2px rules for section heads. Dark theme is a night board: the same signals on near-black, each tuned for contrast.

Type: Archivo only. Condensed widths for every figure (large, tabular in columns), normal width for text. Icons drawn on a 24 grid with square ends.

## Why it is unmistakably money and my month

The band is on every desktop screen and on Home on the phone: money left in the largest type on the screen, 4 days to payday, the amount per day until then, and a strip of the 31 days of the November cycle with today boxed. Spent this cycle is split into Personal, Shared with Anna, and Moved to the shared account (not spending), and the same marks run down every transaction list.

## Least sure of

Whether the hard grid and the full-width green band feel calm enough to open many times a day, and whether the Sankey's large Home flow (rent) crowds the smaller categories on a phone.
