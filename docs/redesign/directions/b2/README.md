# b2: Night Dial

**Idea:** one dial on a night-blue panel: the ring is the cycle's budget, amber is what is left, the ticks around it are the 31 days, and you drag the ring to see how much was left on any day.

## References it builds on

- **Apple Card, interest dial (applecard-05):** a ring you drag to make money tangible, with the figure in the centre. Taken: the draggable ring with the money left in the middle; dragging scrubs back through the cycle's days.
- **YNAB, cover overspending (ynab-05):** the biggest overspend leads as a red card. Taken: Eating out leads Over budget, the others follow as quiet rows.
- **YNAB, dark Reflect (ynab-09) and category pills (ynab-03):** rich dark ground with bold bars and a one-line verdict; available money in a coloured pill. Taken: the dark night palette, the verdict sentence on Analytics, and the amber or red pills in the budget list.
- **Revolut, joint account (revolut-04) and big balance (revolut-02):** a shared balance as a warm object with two people on it. Taken: the shared card with both initials and a big balance, in the cluster's second light (ice blue).
- **Linear, projects and inbox (linear-01, linear-02):** calm dense lists, status in one column. Taken: transaction rows with a bare icon, one status tag (Shared or Personal) and the amount aligned right.
- **Copilot, cash-flow bars (copilot-05):** one bar per period where only the outlier shouts. Taken: Analytics shows change per category; only increases are amber.

## Colour rule and type

- Amber is the instrument light and always means money still left (on the dial, the budget rings, the pills). Red means past the line. Green is money in. Ice blue, a car cluster's second light, is the shared account with Anna. Readings are warm white on night blue in dark, ink on cool grey in light.
- Each category is a small tachometer: the ring is the budget plus a red zone beyond it, so overspending is red ring, not just red text.
- Type: Barlow, from road-sign and instrument lettering. Medium weight big figures, tabular figures in columns. No monospace.

## Why it is unmistakably about money and my month

The ring runs from 30 Oct to Payday; the first half of the ring, marked Fixed, is rent and bills; the day ticks run over the flexible half, so the amber sliver and the Today tick show at once that 827 kr. must last 4 days and that flexible spending is 484 kr. ahead of an even pace. Shared, personal and moved money are tagged on every row and counted separately at the top of Transactions; top-ups are moved money, never spending.

## States and interaction

State switcher in the corner (Data, Loading, Empty, Error) on Cycle, Transactions, Budgets and Analytics. Drag the dial (or focus it and use the arrow keys) to read any day. Add steps amount, category, name, saved, then returns; date, note, shared and income are chips one tap away.

## Least sure of

Whether the rent-and-bills half of the ring reads clearly without a legend, and whether the light theme is rich enough next to the dark one.
