# The owner's design taste: evidence

The owner's own words and marks behind `.agents/skills/ui-ux/taste.md`. They were said while redesigning the owner's other app, CoupleGoAI, and hold for any app; that app's own look (its colours, mark, pictures) is left out on purpose and does not apply to Cashflow. Cashflow's own words go in a section of their own below as the owner gives them.

Collected on 2026-11-26 from the owner's messages in Claude and Codex sessions (5 to 26 Nov 2026), the research-board marks, the direction pages' notes, the redesign handoff (`FEEDBACK.md`, `OWNER.md`) and agent memory. Quotes are short and verbatim (dictation errors kept); dates are 2026.

Use this file to check a rule, not as a rule book: the skill files are the rules. Add to it only with the owner's words.

## Sources

- **Chat messages:** the owner dictates; transcripts of the Claude and Codex sessions on this Mac.
- **Research board** (23 Nov): 109 screens from 36 apps and our own; 63 loved, 41 hated, with notes. Raw data in the design handoff on the owner's Mac: `design-handoff/2026-11-23-redesign/artifact-data/research-board/`.
- **Direction pages** (23 Nov): notes on eight directions (`artifact-data/eight-directions/`).
- **Redesign feedback** (25 Nov): `design-handoff/2026-11-23-redesign/implementation/FEEDBACK.md`, F1 to F27, the owner's words per item.

## General (any app)

**Slop and what fails**
- "we use cheap gradients, cheap buttons. It doesn't look premium on Apple device" (13 Nov)
- "No, the gradients is awful." (25 Nov); "Very stupid chip gradients, very stupid elements" (23 Nov, Glow direction)
- "it seems like you used [...] Gemini or something to generate this, so they don't look real" (25 Nov)
- "It looks like good apps, but just as an app. It still should be very clear that this is all about relationships" (23 Nov)
- "too many options, buttons views, looks like google docs [...] it is cluttered" (23 Nov, board)
- "Too many colors. Looks to childish" (23 Nov, board, Couple Joy Home)
- "it feels a bit weird that we have things in a different style, even when it comes to rounding" (8 Nov)
- "It looks like the material colors from Jetpack Compose been used, and I don't want to have this feeling" (25 Nov, app icon); website "a bit less material" (25 Nov)
- "There is too much text in it. It must be visual." (25 Nov, onboarding)
- "the hand one, why it's 4 fingers, not 5 [...] The key icon is also stupid" (25 Nov)
- Reports and decks: "no AI slop, no AI bullets or AI explained labels or badges, just normal report" (8 Nov); "No ai slop in presentstion no stupid badges or over exained text" (24 Nov); "'Founder pitch' and the middle dot this exactly what I call ai slop.!!!" (24 Nov); "I read it as cheap gradient and bad conteast" (24 Nov)

**What is good**
- "we should aim for Apple-like design without copying them. [...] the same idea, but the figures should be different" (13 Nov)
- "it should be super intuitive when you look at each single point" (14 Nov); "they should still be very easy to understand" (25 Nov)
- "if somebody notices widget on their friend's phone, they're like, wow, what is that actually? [...] I want this" (25 Nov)
- Board loves: "love the creative look" (Tolan, Finch, Headspace, Candle, Brilliant, Duolingo, Gentler Streak); "love the creative and natural shapes" (Monument Valley); Airbnb "just looks like perfect example of 'super good' UX"; Apple Books "very good UI and UX"; Couple Joy's card fan "looks very simple and proffessional, good choreogrpahy!" (23 Nov)
- "we want to move away from having from being perceived as AI app [...] boiling and hand-drawn elements are nice" (25 Nov)
- "very simple, nice animations, nothing crazy" (25 Nov); "it must be full of animations, full of fun, full of interactions, full of like unique experiences" (23 Nov)
- Old widgets: "this grained look, this bright, almost neon colors, no cheap gradients, like small lighted points [...] it was just made professionally" (25 Nov)

**Process**
- "I'm a visual type of person, so you need to always build some kind of artifact where I can see what you propose" (23 Nov)
- "make them more interactive and more clickable, so that I can actually almost like use an app" (23 Nov)
- "It's too much text to read for me. Can you maybe assume that I'm a little bit ADHD [...] and make it a bit more visual" (25 Nov)
- Choices page: "so easy to read [...] whenever you prototype or I ask you for visual directions [...] you do it in this style" (25 Nov)
- "Don't overreview it. Let me handle that. Just a small superficial review is enough" (24 Nov); "fed up with all these minor fixes" (23 Nov)
- Handoff rules (23 Nov): "Never remove a feature or look the owner might like without asking." "Never grade your own work with your own rules." "Show the old screens beside the new ones for the whole app."
- "I expect you first to prototype and give me three variants [...] and then I choose one, and then you will implement it exactly like I want it to be" (13 Nov)
- "you must utilize all these best skills out there, like Apple design, taste, impeccable [...] that, first of all, don't introduce AI slop, and second of all, actually bring something creative" (23 Nov)
- "explore all the flows without any shortcuts. Just as a normal user would [...] but also try to be a little bit like Q&A" (26 Nov)
